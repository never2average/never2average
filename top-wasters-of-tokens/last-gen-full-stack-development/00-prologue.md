# Last-Generation Full-Stack Development

Building the previous generation of full-stack apps is documented, repetitive work, and it is where the largest share of tokens currently goes. The process was already written down before the models arrived; paying frontier inference to rediscover it every time is the waste.

## Open questions

- What part of a full-stack build is genuinely new work, and what is recall?
- What would it cost to generate this app once and never pay for it again?
