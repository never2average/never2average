# Moulds and the Vibe-Coding Factory

A mould is the reusable shape of a product, not the product. The factory idea is that once the shape is right, instances should come out of it cheaply and repeatedly, with the expensive judgment spent on the mould rather than on each cast.

## Open questions

- What belongs in the mould, and what has to stay bespoke per instance?
- How does a mould improve without invalidating everything already cast from it?
